THE PAGES INSIDE

A little room for each part of the day.

Real Daymark screens. Sample details are labeled so an example never looks like your own day.

01 PLAN

Start with today.

The next event comes first. Tasks, rituals, and the shape of the day sit close at hand, without turning the page into a crowded dashboard.

Add a task or check in on a ritual from Today.

02 PRACTICE

Keep a practice, without the scoreboard.

Set a daily, weekday, or weekly rhythm. The ledger shows your check-ins across the week; a missed day is information, not a reason to start over.

Check in from Today or from the Rituals page.

03 FOCUS

Give your attention a place to land.

Start a focus session, choose a length, or use the short and long rests. Your focus history keeps today’s sessions and the last seven days together.

The running session stays in view as you move to another page.

04 REVIEW

Look back at what happened.

The weekly page gathers tasks, focus, and rituals you recorded. Prompts help you notice what mattered, what got in the way, and one change to try next.

Review uses the daybook you made. It does not invent progress.

05 SHELF

Keep what you want to find again.

Save a note, link, or image to the Shelf. Share something in from another app, then come back to it when the day has more room.

Search, filter, tag, and pin the things you keep.

06 ASK DAYMARK

Ask about the day you actually have.

Ask Daymark can answer from a read-only view of your daybook and turn loose phrasing into a typed, reviewable action. The optional Gemma model is a separate download you choose to install; it runs on your device.

The model is optional. Web search is a separate setting, off by default, and sends only your question when you use it.

A WIDER PAGE

The daybook opens up on iPad.

Plan and Practice sit across an open spread, with the day’s events and tasks on the other page. The same pages adapt to a regular iPhone layout.

A CLEAR EDGE

Private by default. Specific about the exceptions.

Daymark has no account, analytics, cloud sync, or remote AI service.

01

Your daybook stays on your device.

Tasks, events, rituals, focus, reviews, and Shelf entries live in the app on your device.

02

Optional means you choose.

Search is off by default. The on-device model downloads only when you request it.

03

System sync stays yours.

Calendar and Reminders use the system accounts and lists you select; any account sync follows that provider’s settings.
