SUPPORT NOTES

A few things worth knowing.

Daymark is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad. These answers describe the current app. This preview does not publish a direct support email address.

Getting oriented

What is Daymark?

A local-first daybook for planning the day, keeping rituals, focusing, collecting notes and links, and reviewing the week. Its five main pages are Today, Rituals, Focus, Review, and Shelf.

Does Daymark start with sample data?

A fresh install starts empty. Sample content is optional and labeled where it appears. You can choose Start fresh in the app to clear the sample daybook.

Does Daymark sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Daymark does not provide cloud sync for its daybook. You can export and import a limited set of records. If you connect Apple Calendar or Reminders, those system accounts may sync the selected calendars or lists according to that provider’s settings.

Data and connections

What does a daybook export contain?

Tasks, events, Shelf records, and sample-data metadata. It does not include rituals and check-ins, focus history and timers, weekly reviews, assistant chat, or Shelf images. Daily restore copies cover the same limited file. See the privacy details for the complete scope.

Does Ask Daymark need the model download?

No. The optional Gemma model adds local language interpretation and open-question answers. It is downloaded only when you choose it and runs on your device. Web search is a separate option, off by default; if you enable and use it, only the question is sent for search.

What happens when I connect Calendar or Reminders?

You choose which calendars and lists Daymark can read. Changes to imported items can be written back through Apple’s system services. Any account sync is governed by the accounts and settings on your device, not by a Daymark cloud service.

Where can I read the privacy details?

The Privacy page explains local storage, optional network features, Calendar and Reminders, and export limits.
